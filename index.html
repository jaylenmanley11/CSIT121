Baltimore Ravens Website Evaluation

CSIT 121 - Lab 05

Website Information

Website URL: https://www.baltimoreravens.com/

Website name: Baltimore Ravens Official Website

Target audience: The target audience includes Ravens fans, NFL fans, and people who want team news! It also includes people looking for tickets, schedules, videos, player info, etc.

Website Organization

The website is mainly organized hierarchically. The main navigation has broad topics that lead to more specific pages. The home page also shows news and other content in groups as users scroll down.

CRAP Design Principles

CRAP stands for Contrast, Repetition, Alignment, and Proximity.

  • Contrast: The Ravens' purple and black colors stand out against lighter backgrounds.
  • Repetition: News stories and videos use similar layouts, so users can recognize similar content.
  • Alignment: Text and pictures are arranged in organized rows and sections.
  • Proximity: Related pictures, headlines, and links are placed near each other.

One example is the repeated layout of news stories, which helps visitors scan the page.

Accessibility

The Ravens have an accessibility statement on their website.

Accessibility Checker audit score: 10/100 Ravens website Accessibility Checker.

Website Screenshot

Website Effectiveness

The website is pretty effective for fans who want to find team news, videos, schedules and more.

Website Efficiency

The navigation/page layouts can help visitors find familiar information quickly. However, there is a lot of content on the home page, so users may need to scroll to find a particular story or detail.

Engagement

The site is engaging for football fans because it uses team colors, photos, videos, and current stories. These features fit the football theme and help fans stay interested. The page should still be easy to read and use.

Recommendation

I would add alternative text to images, so screen readers can explain what the images show to people who are visually impaired.
